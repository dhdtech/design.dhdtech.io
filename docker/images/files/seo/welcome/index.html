DHDTech.io Design

A free, self-hosted instance of Penpot — the open-source design and prototyping platform — hosted by DHDTech.io.

What it is

DHDTech.io Design runs in your browser. You open it, sign in, and start designing: interfaces, screens, and interactive prototypes, built on the free and open-source Penpot toolset.

Who it is for

Designers, product teams, and anyone who needs a design and prototyping tool without a per-seat subscription. Open source is the point: nothing here is locked to a vendor, and your work is yours.

Free, and self-hosted by DHDTech.io

This instance is hosted and paid for by DHDTech.io. There is no charge to use it and no trial that expires — it is free software, running on our servers.
